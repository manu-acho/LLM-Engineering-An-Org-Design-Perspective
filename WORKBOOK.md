## The cumulative output

The pilot ends with a short design for a delivery-investigation service. It should identify the problem, available evidence, unresolved claims, technical contribution, recipient, permitted next step and outcome measures. The quantities and business conditions are fictional.

1. After Lesson 1.1, draw the boundary between model and application.
2. After Lesson 1.2, identify a function's input, output and possible failure.
3. After Lesson 1.3, calculate the learned probabilities after `the`.
4. After Lesson 1.4, trace one source identifier through the prepared brief and request.
5. After Lesson 1.5, explain what the tiny model leaves out of a neural LLM.
6. After Lesson 1.6, compare lookup, drafting and specialist conversation.
7. After Lesson 1.7, test the design against an uncertain quantity and a missing owner.

The exercises and worked responses are in the lessons. A successful numerical answer is one part of the output. The design should also preserve the uncertainty and identify the work that remains for people and other services.

## A completed first design

**Problem:** operations needs a usable account of a reported delivery discrepancy. **Evidence:** the order records twelve boxes, the shipment record records twelve, and the customer reports ten. **Unresolved:** actual receipt and cause. **Technical contribution:** a source-labelled brief that preserves the disagreement. **Recipient:** an investigator who can obtain clarification and inspect the records. **Boundary:** the assistant cannot confirm a cause or issue a credit. **Correction route:** the investigator can revise a claim with its source, and the receiving team can return incomplete evidence. **Measures:** unsupported claims, preparation and checking effort, clarification and elapsed resolution time.

This is a proposed arrangement, not evidence that Harbour has implemented it or achieved a saving. The first comparison is structured intake with direct lookup versus the same intake with a draft. That comparison helps separate the contribution of generation from better evidence collection.
