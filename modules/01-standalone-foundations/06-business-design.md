## The service must do more than prepare text

**Fictional worked case.** Harbour wants faster delivery investigations. Preparation is one task. Customer clarification, shipment checking, interpretation, approval and communication are other contributions. A faster brief can leave the overall case waiting if those contributions are poorly connected.

**Differentiation** means dividing work into specialised tasks and roles. **Integration** means bringing those contributions together. Burton and Obel's organisation design argument explains why the relationship between structure and coordination matters. An assistant should be examined as a contribution within that arrangement. [Organisation design chapter](../../reference/research/01-organisation-design.md#division-creates-a-need-for-integration)

## Diagnose the dependency

Operations depends on a usable account from intake. This is a sequential dependency: one contribution becomes another activity's input. A disputed count can create reciprocal dependence as the customer-facing team and warehouse revise their accounts in response to each other. The same case can contain both.

A structured brief may improve a stable handoff. Repeated clarification may require a conversation. A queue introduces a different dependency when several cases compete for investigator attention. These problems should be identified before adding more agent roles. [Coordination chapter](../../reference/research/02-coordination.md#identify-what-depends-on-what)

## Compare three arrangements

| Arrangement | Contribution | Remaining organisational requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Structured intake and direct lookup | Show the source-labelled facts and difference | Investigator interprets uncertainty and obtains clarification |
| Referenced model draft | Express a usable brief from permitted evidence | Reviewer checks claims, rejects errors and chooses a next step |
| Specialist conversation | Clarify conflicting meanings or interests | Participants need time, expertise and accepted responsibility |

The choices can coexist for different cases. A model draft should earn its place against a simpler comparator. A polished answer alone does not show that generation is the bottleneck or the best response.

## Locate judgement and authority

A decision premise is a fact, category, goal or rule that helps structure a choice. Labelling a record `reported` rather than `confirmed` preserves a premise the investigator needs. Selecting only one of the conflicting records changes the basis of the choice before any final approval.

A reviewer needs more than a button. Source access, relevant knowledge, time and the practical ability to disagree affect meaningful review. A technical permission does not by itself establish organisational authority or effective influence. [Decision chapter](../../reference/research/03-decisions.md#permission-and-authority)

Harbour's first design therefore limits the assistant to preparation. The investigator can inspect evidence and return an incomplete brief. A warehouse owner checks shipment information; the customer-facing team obtains a recount where necessary. A service owner addresses stalled cases. These are proposed responsibilities to test against actual working conditions.

## Calculate a benefit without overstating it

Suppose 300 monthly cases are eligible and the draft is used on 70%, or 210 cases. Preparation falls from twelve to seven minutes, while checking adds two minutes. Net effort reduction is three minutes per use, or 630 minutes: 10.5 hours a month.

At an illustrative labour cost of £30 an hour, that capacity is valued at £315. If monthly running and support costs are £180, the scenario difference is £135 before implementation costs. These are teaching assumptions, not measured savings. Released capacity may support other work rather than reduce expenditure.

If review takes five extra minutes, the preparation gain disappears. If warehouse waiting remains a day, a shorter preparation task may leave elapsed resolution unchanged. The evaluation should record preparation, checking, clarification, waiting, unsupported claims and work shifted to other roles. [Implementation and benefit evidence](../../reference/research/04-implementation.md#benefits-need-a-population-and-a-comparison)

## Follow the work beyond its diagram

A workflow and brief are artefacts: representations and arrangements used in work. They do not establish the entire recurrent routine. Staff may use calls, spreadsheets or local knowledge to repair gaps. A useful inquiry studies several cases and asks why such activities occur before treating every deviation as error. [Routines chapter](../../reference/research/05-routines.md#what-logs-can-show)

The same source panel may enable one investigator to challenge a claim while offering little to someone without access or time. An affordance is a possibility for a particular actor to act through an arrangement under relevant conditions. Its actualisation requires action and supporting conditions over time. [Technology in practice](../../reference/research/06-technology-in-practice.md#feature-affordance-and-constraint)

## A first pilot decision

Harbour proposes a bounded comparison for long, non-urgent cases: improved structured intake with direct lookup, versus the same intake with a referenced draft. Source and correction ownership are explicit. Uncertain cases can remain open, and disputed meanings have a conversation route. Expansion depends on observed quality and useful service or capacity consequences.

The design does not yet justify automatic credits, agent teams or cloud deployment. Those are further choices requiring a specific contribution and evidence. The planned operational part of the course develops the requirements, controls and continuing ownership needed as a service becomes more consequential.
