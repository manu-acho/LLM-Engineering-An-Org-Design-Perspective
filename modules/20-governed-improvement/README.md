# Module 20: Governed improvement and integration capstone

[Curriculum](../../CURRICULUM.md) · [Setup](../../STUDY-WORKSPACE.md) · [Glossary](../../reference/glossary.md)

An improvement loop can optimise a proxy while degrading the service. Harbour treats a proposed configuration as a candidate that must pass comparable evaluation, explicit safety and cost conditions, and a human promotion decision.

## Harbour at this point

Harbour’s final decision concerns a proposed business arrangement as well as a software configuration. A candidate can match more route references while consuming more resources or increasing review work. The company must weigh those trade-offs and retain the option to simplify, defer or withdraw the capability.

**Supply-chain and organisation design question:** Produce a final business and organisation design proposal joining customer outcome, goods and information flows, finance authority, service ownership and measured technical evidence.

Read [Meet Harbour](../../HARBOUR.md) for the company and cross-functional workflow, or revisit [this module’s place in the case](../../reference/harbour-learning-journey.md#module-20). The technical activity examines a selected contribution; it does not establish that the proposed arrangement has been implemented.

## Included code and learning boundary

The original implementation is `src/ai_orgdesign/labs/m20.py`. The notebooks import that code so the explanations and checked behaviour share one implementation. The core route runs locally without paid services. Optional integrations are explicitly gated and described in the [verification guide](../../reference/verification.md).

The illustrative candidate is blocked without approval and passes the stated gate with approval. A candidate with more unsafe decisions is blocked even if its accuracy improves. Different dataset identities raise an error because the comparison is not the declared experiment. Promotion does not establish statistical significance or guarantee future performance.

## Completion exercise

Suppose an improver repeatedly tests candidates against the same held-out cases. Explain why those cases eventually stop functioning as an independent test, even if their filenames never change.

A satisfactory response explains the mechanism, interprets the observed output, identifies the relevant authority and source ownership, compares an alternative and states what additional evidence would support a business claim. The worked answers are included in the workshop and design lessons. Continue with [organisation design research](../../reference/research/05-routines.md) for the supporting theory and scholarly references.

## Demonstrate the learning

- Measure the current and candidate instruction paths on the same cases.
- Require explicit approval and comparable evaluation before promotion.
- Keep a rollback target without rewriting immutable versions.
- Explain the case-specific acceptance, authority, correction and evaluation arrangements.
