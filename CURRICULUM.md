# Standalone curriculum

[Course home](README.md) · [Start here](START-HERE.md)

The curriculum integrates foundations, agent engineering and business operations. The first module is a completed standalone pilot. The remaining entries define the intended learning path; their complete standalone lessons and code have not yet been written. Existing companion-edition files retained in the development checkout are not part of this published collection.

## How the parts build on one another

Retrieval, evaluation, state and tools are introduced once, then revisited in more demanding service settings. Part III extends their application into requirements, support, controls and continuing change. It does not repeat the earlier sequence under new course labels.

The research chapters connect the parts: task division creates integration work; categories and rules structure decisions; workflow diagrams do not establish recurrent practice; deployment does not establish benefit. The final capstone will require technical evidence and a defensible design for the surrounding work.
