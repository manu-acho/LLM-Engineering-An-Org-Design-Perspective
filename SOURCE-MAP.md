## Engineering influences

Ed Donner's three courses provide important influences on the choice of engineering topics and the progression from models to agents and operational services:

- [LLM Engineering](https://github.com/ed-donner/llm_engineering): generation, retrieval, evaluation, data and adaptation.
- [Agents](https://github.com/ed-donner/agents): loops, orchestration, frameworks, state and capability interfaces.
- [AI FDE: The Forward Deployed Engineer & Agentic AI Architect](https://github.com/ed-donner/undercover): business requirements, harnesses, evaluation, durable work, security, enterprise context, deployment and controlled improvement.

These courses are optional further learning. The course does not require their videos, code, datasets or accounts. Credit for the original courses and repository contributions belongs to Ed Donner and their credited contributors. This edition does not imply affiliation or endorsement.

## Original examples

The model exercises, evidence preparation, agent patterns and service implementations were written for this course. They use fictional cases and quantities, with checks that make their behaviour inspectable. Each example explains what runs, what its results show and where their limits lie.

## Scholarly foundation

Six [research chapters](reference/literature-guide.md) explain the organisation design concepts within the course and cite the scholarly publications. Their cases are fictional. Concepts from enterprise-system, healthcare and other studies are applied with their original setting boundaries intact.

## Teaching scope and verification

All twenty module routes include their own teaching material and local implementations. The [verification guide](reference/verification.md) distinguishes actual execution, toy-model boundaries, optional paid calls and unexecuted deployment conditions. Source-course prices, benchmark figures and claimed outcomes are not adopted as results of this course.
